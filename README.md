1)

*Text to input at Step 1*: "A city wants to build a $500m stadium, on a much larger scale than any project it has delivered before. The city has limited in-house construction experience. Environmental groups have raised concerns about habitats of endangered animals on the proposed site, and regulators will need to approve the environmental impact. The city wants the stadium open in time for a major sporting event in four years’ time, and the funding is partly from state government and partly from a private partner who wants cost certainty. The design isn’t finalised, and the city wants to keep a say in how the stadium looks and what it includes."

*Question to input at Step 5*: "Why is the top-ranked method a better fit than traditional construct only, given that the city wants to keep a say in the design but the design isn’t finalised?"

2)

*Text to input at Step 1*: "A private school wants to build a new four-classroom block on its existing campus. The architect has already completed the full design and documentation, and the school’s facilities manager is experienced and wants to approve all the design and material choices. The school has plenty of time and wants the lowest competitive price through a tender. The work is straightforward, with no unusual site conditions, and there are no outside stakeholders other than the school board."

*Question to input at Step 5*: "What would change if the design weren’t finished and the school wanted to start building early?"

3)
